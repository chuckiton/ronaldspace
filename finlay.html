The grammar

1

Body

The first variable chooses an unmistakable skeleton: ring, cross, cleft, or staff.

2

Intervention

The second truncates, grafts, sets orientation, or bars the skeleton. Orientation is drawn as given—never animated as a turn.

3

Witness

A detached mark records the third: arc, saltire, wedge, or score. It never interrupts the route.

4

Cadence

The ur-form decides how the ligature grows; the fourth changes only its ductus: taut, bowed, counter-bowed, or inflected.
